## Research: Free offline map apps for travelling abroad

Angle: the reader is about to travel and does not want to depend on mobile data.
- DISCARDED: an app whose free plan capped offline downloads at a few regions. Useless for a multi-city trip.
- HONESTY: none of these replace live traffic; say so.

### Organic Maps
- Free offline maps for walking, cycling and driving, built on OpenStreetMap data, with no ads.
- VERIFIED on organicmaps.app: free, open source, maps downloaded by country or region.
- CAVEAT: no live traffic, and map detail depends on how well OpenStreetMap covers that area.

### Google Maps offline areas
- Download an area before the trip, then search and drive in it without mobile data.
- VERIFIED in Google Maps Help: offline maps are downloaded per area and used when there is no connection.
- CAVEAT: download on Wi-Fi before leaving; transit, cycling and walking directions may not work offline.

## Research: Remove the hidden location from photos before sharing

Angle: a photo can carry the exact spot where it was taken. The reader sends photos every day.
- DISCARDED: an online metadata remover that uploads the photo to its server. Uploading the photo defeats the point of protecting it.
- HONESTY: messaging apps behave differently for photos and documents; do not promise one rule for all.

### iPhone share options
- When sharing a photo, tap Options at the top of the share sheet and switch Location off.
- VERIFIED in Apple Support: the share sheet Options menu can leave location out of the shared copy.
- CAVEAT: it applies to that share only; the photo in your library keeps its location.

### Windows file properties
- Right-click the photo, open Properties, then Details, then Remove Properties and Personal Information.
- VERIFIED on Windows 11 File Explorer.
- CAVEAT: choose the option that creates a clean copy if you want to keep the original untouched.
